1. Certified theorem

theorem twoColor_oddSupport_moment_zero
    {V : Type*} [DecidableEq V]
    (color : V → Bool) (S : Finset V)
    (heven : Even S.card) (hodd : OddOnSupport color S) :
    twoColorExpectationLinear
      (fun o =>
        twoColorValue false o ^ colorMultiplicity color S false *
        twoColorValue true o ^ colorMultiplicity color S true) = 0

In mathematical notation, if \(S\) has even size and some Boolean color occurs an odd number of times, then

\[\mathbb E\prod_{x\in S}Z_{c(x)}=0\]

for the explicit three-atom law with outcomes \((1,1),(1,-1),(-1,1)\) and probabilities \(1/2,1/4,1/4\).

3. Proof decomposition

Probability

twoColor_mean and twoColor_pair_zero enumerate three atoms exactly. No floating point is used.

Parity

both_odd_of_even_sum_of_one_odd proves that an even total and one odd class force both classes odd.

Sign powers

sign_pow_eq_self_of_odd reduces odd powers of each sign to the sign itself.

Finite support

bool_colorMultiplicity_sum proves that the two Boolean color classes partition the support cardinality.

5. Trust boundary

Inside

The finite distributions, algebraic identities, parity implications, sharp polynomial inequalities, and Riesz-factor nonnegativity are checked by Lean's kernel.

Outside

The project does not formalize the full Fourier expansion of the covering function, Bonami–Rudin progression estimates, construction and odd-coloring of the arithmetic relation hypergraph, or the conditional \(n^{-3/2+o(1)}\) transfer. It does not certify the Lonely Runner Conjecture.

6. Repository policy

The theorem files use no proof placeholders. Continuous integration builds the project, runs leanchecker, and audits axiom dependencies. Future extensions should keep theorem, conditional deduction, and open problem separate: a theorem with an unproved hypothesis is only as strong as the hypothesis it exposes.
