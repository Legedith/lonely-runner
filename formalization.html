Formalization guide

Lean checks that odd coloring is not merely sufficient: on every even support it is equivalent to exact zero moment under the explicit three-atom law. The analytic Lonely Runner transfer remains outside the certified boundary.

1. Main certified theorem

theorem twoColor_oddSupport_iff_moment_zero
    {V : Type*} [DecidableEq V]
    (color : V → Bool) (S : Finset V) (heven : Even S.card) :
    OddOnSupport color S ↔
      twoColorExpectationLinear
        (fun o =>
          twoColorValue false o ^ colorMultiplicity color S false *
          twoColorValue true o ^ colorMultiplicity color S true) = 0

For the three-atom law with outcomes \((1,1),(1,-1),(-1,1)\) and probabilities \(1/2,1/4,1/4\), Lean proves

\[\mathbb E\prod_{x\in S}Z_{c(x)}=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\exists i\in\{0,1\}:\#\{x\in S:c(x)=i\}\text{ is odd},\]

whenever \(|S|\) is even. The reverse direction is important: if the support is not odd-colored, both color counts are even and the mixed moment is exactly one.

3. Proof decomposition

Probability

twoColor_mean and twoColor_pair_zero enumerate the three atoms exactly. No floating point is used.

Parity

both_odd_of_even_sum_of_one_odd proves that an even total and one odd class force both classes odd.

Both parity cases

twoColor_odd_odd_moment_zero gives zero; twoColor_even_even_moment_one gives one. Together they make the criterion exact.

Finite support

bool_colorMultiplicity_sum proves that the two Boolean color classes partition the support cardinality.

5. What formal verification does and does not establish

Inside the kernel-checked boundary

The finite distributions, exact if-and-only-if cancellation theorem, parity implications, sharp polynomial inequalities, moment factorization, and Riesz-factor nonnegativity are checked by Lean's kernel.

Outside the boundary

The project does not formalize the full Fourier expansion of the covering function, Bonami–Rudin progression estimates, construction and odd-coloring of the arithmetic relation hypergraph, or the conditional \(n^{-3/2+o(1)}\) transfer. It does not certify the Lonely Runner Conjecture. Lean also cannot certify historical novelty; that claim rests on literature review.

6. Repository policy

The theorem files use no proof placeholders. Continuous integration builds the project, runs leanchecker, and audits axiom dependencies. The project keeps three categories separate: machine-checked theorem, conditional analytic deduction, and open arithmetic hypothesis.
