The central theorem

Exact cancellation from one parity bit

Let \((Z_0,Z_1)\) take the following three values. This is a correlated probability law, not an independence assumption.

OutcomeProbability
\((1,1)\)\(1/2\)
\((1,-1)\)\(1/4\)
\((-1,1)\)\(1/4\)
Finite cancellation theorem

For nonnegative integers \(m,n\), if \(m+n\) is even and at least one of \(m,n\) is odd, then

\[\mathbb E[Z_0^mZ_1^n]=0.\]

Also \(\mathbb EZ_0=\mathbb EZ_1=1/2\). Thus the law retains half of the weighted first-level mass while killing the designated even-support monomial exactly.

Evenness of \(m+n\) and oddness of one summand force both summands to be odd. Since each \(Z_i\) is a sign, \(Z_i^m=Z_i\). Direct evaluation gives \(\mathbb E[Z_0Z_1]=0\). □

Why odd coloring appears

Relations form a hypergraph

In a Riesz-product expansion, each harmful low-order additive relation has a finite support. Regard supports as hyperedges and color their frequencies with two colors. If an even hyperedge has a color occurring an odd number of times, both color multiplicities are odd, so the theorem cancels that relation.

1

Encode

Vertices are selected Fourier frequencies. Hyperedges are supports of the short relations producing unwanted terms.

2

Odd-color

Every even hyperedge must have a nonzero parity vector: some color occurs an odd number of times.

3

Cancel

Pull the two-color law back through the coloring. The corresponding even-level moment is exactly zero.

Boundary. The formal theorem proves this finite cancellation interface. It does not prove that every Lonely Runner relation hypergraph has a suitable odd coloring, nor does it formalize all Fourier-analytic tail estimates.
Formal verification

What the kernel checks

Two-color law

Normalization, nonnegative weights, sign bounds, common mean \(1/2\), zero pair moment, and the odd–odd moment theorem.

Odd-color parity

An even support with an odd Boolean color class has both class sizes odd and therefore zero mixed moment.

Scalar filters

Explicit filters of mean \(1/3\) and \(1/5\) kill the third, or third and fifth, moments. Matching sharp upper bounds are proved.

Riesz positivity

For \(0\le\rho\le1\) and \(|u|\le1\), each factor \(1-\rho u\cos\theta\) and every finite product is nonnegative.

Research status

A new interface, not a solved conjecture

Established here

A self-contained probability law and parity theorem turning an odd coloring of even relation supports into exact moment cancellation, with compatible scalar filters and positivity lemmas.

Still required

An arithmetic theorem controlling the odd chromatic number of the actual short-relation hypergraph, plus a complete analytic transfer into a global Lonely Runner bound.

Odd coloring itself is established terminology. The apparently new point is its use as the exact combinatorial interface for correlated amplitudes in a Lonely Runner Riesz product, combined with independent odd-moment filters. A targeted public search through 31 August 2026 found no earlier instance of this synthesis; that is evidence, not an absolute priority guarantee.
